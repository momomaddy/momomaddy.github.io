Methodology

Three principles.
No corporate wallpaper.

01 / SIGNAL

Find the signal.

Most organisations have far more interesting stories than their press releases suggest. You have to dig past boilerplate memos to uncover the contrarian detail that makes an editor or investor pay attention.

02 / EMBEDDED TRUTH

Connect the business to the audience.

A solar transmission corridor, airport concourse, financial product, or industrial facility only becomes meaningful when people can see where it fits into their everyday lives.

03 / VELOCITY

Make the story travel.

The right strategic idea should work seamlessly across environments—landing with equal authority in a boardroom, a newsroom, an executive LinkedIn feed, and a phone screen.
